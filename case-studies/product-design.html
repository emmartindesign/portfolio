Overview

Sourcescrub is a private-company deal-sourcing app which serves private equity firms and banks. I was responsible for the overall ideation and design—including responsiveness and building components for the new layout in Figma and in Code.

As of Nov 27, 2024, this project has not been launched, but is approved and awaiting development.

Goal

Sourcescrub's primary workflow involves users searching for companies and reviewing available data in company profiles. Often, hundreds of company profiles are reviewed. Being able to quickly absorb as much information as possible is paramount, and the original page layout was not delivering.

The goal was to overhaul the original layout—make it responsive for core screen sizes, and place all of the most pertinent information "above the fold."

The redesigned bento-style company data profile layout.

Data collection

The primary goal of the reorganization of data was to review all the currently available data and identify with customers what the most pertinent data points are when they're doing quick reviews of a company.

  • Which stats are vital to know?
  • What workflows need to have clear signals (has the company been tagged, synced to a CRM, etc.)?
  • On longer data points, like descriptions, how much of it is important to show?
  • Which actions are vital to have at one's fingertips?
  • What formatting is required for data to be most effective?

Current legacy layout shown to the right.

Original desktop layout before the bento overhaul (scroll to explore).

We discovered that our users wanted to see as much as possible in a glance—stats written in clear language, an overview of the business model, and more. They didn't want to scroll at all for the top-level info... and there's a lot of top-level info.

Business model

Users want to know what a business is about—its industry, customers, and description.

Their financial profile

Users want to know if a company is doing well financially and which firms have already invested.

Their previous interactions

Users want to know if they've looked at this company in the past, or if someone in their firm has, and if so, what they decided at that time.

Who to contact

If a company is a good match, users want to reach out to the top contact ASAP.

Design

We needed to increase the responsiveness of the layout and provide more definition between subsections of data. There are plans to implement a mobile version of the profile, and many customers are on large screens. The bento style of dashboard seemed to be the best option for achieving all of the responsiveness goals.

Components

With the design approved, the next step was to create Angular components the team could use to build the experience. The primary component was a bento-style tile.

The story for the Bento tile in Storybook.

This change is slated to release in the next couple of months, but the design choices made here will shape the Sourcescrub app across the board.
