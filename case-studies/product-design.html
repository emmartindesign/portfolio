Selected projects (2020 – Present)

Overview

A successful product is far more than dashboards and tables. When product-market fit exists, users rely on dependable data presented through streamlined, predictable interactions.

Goal

Deliver intuitive digital products that remove friction from complex domain tasks, turning cumbersome business operations into repeatable, confidence-building workflows.

Research & Discovery

By conducting contextual user interviews, analyzing quantitative session metrics, and mapping journey touchpoints, customer pain points are translated directly into high-impact product priorities.

Interactive Prototyping

Rather than relying solely on static Figma mocks, high-fidelity prototypes and code mockups are used to validate ergonomics, micro-interactions, and real data density with users before engineering investment.

Execution & Measurement

Working side-by-side with developers, products are built to strict design system specifications, with success measured by task completion rates, decreased support tickets, and sustained adoption.

Great products anticipate user intent and get out of the way.
