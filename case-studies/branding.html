Overview

Before focusing on product design, I spent years designing marketing collateral for Technomic, a B2B information-services company focused on foodservice.

Goal

Create visual campaigns that could move across print, digital, trade-show, and sales contexts while staying distinctive and easy to recognize.

Delivery insights campaign

For a March Madness campaign, athlete and food imagery were combined into energetic compositions. Stock images were edited, backgrounds and logos were removed, and the final work ran in print, online, animated banners, and trade-show displays.

Food trends publication

Food and ingredient imagery was carefully researched or created through photo editing and photoshoots. The “eats” logo was hand-drawn and digitized in Illustrator, with animated versions created for digital displays.

Education conference branding

For Winsight’s Menu Directions conference, the concept of a knowledge pillar separated the annual theme from the marketing focus through color, hand-drawn lines, and a flexible visual system for repeatable ads.

Pure visuals, built to travel across media.
