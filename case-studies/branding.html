What's this about?

Before focusing on product design, I spent years designing marketing collateral for Technomic, a B2B information services company focused on foodservice. This is a collection of some of the things I created.

For each of these projects, I served as art director, visual designer, and often copy editor.

Delivery insights campaign

High-energy visual campaign connecting collegiate basketball excitement with foodservice delivery trends.

Campaign ad – Fast break delivery

Campaign ad – Precision passing & packaging

Campaign ad – Game time takeout

The idea

Technomic would often release marketing that corresponded with March Madness—an important season for many foodservice operators when it comes to delivery. To align these ideas, the concept of combining basketball and food imagery emerged.

The creation

The athlete and food images were sourced from stock images with a concerted effort to select a diverse group of individuals. The basketballs, backgrounds, and identifying logos were removed. Food imagery was added to the composition with angles and placement to imply motion. The typography was placed to feel a bit like another player on the court.

The media

These were run in foodservice related magazines both in print and online. Animated banners were created for digital applications, including trade show displays.

Food trends publication

A tactile, premium quarterly trend report highlighting emerging flavors and ingredients.

Publication cover – Custom hand-drawn “eats” logotype

Editorial spread – Ingredient deep dive

Editorial spread – Flavor forecasting

Editorial spread – Consumer adoption curve

The idea

Technomic wanted to tout its far-reaching trend tracking in ingredients and flavors with eye-catching media which could be distributed at trade shows and left with clients in sales meetings.

The creation

Images were carefully researched. Where stock images were unavailable, photo editing would be used to create something identical, or an ingredient would be sourced for a photoshoot. The “eats” in the logo was hand-drawn, then digitized in Adobe Illustrator.

The media

These publications were run on magazine-grade stock with a satin finish. Animated versions of the cover were created for digital displays at trade shows.

Education conference branding

A reusable campaign architecture for Menu Directions, Winsight’s annual public-sector conference.

Print ad – Knowledge pillar thematic variation 1

Print ad – Knowledge pillar thematic variation 2

Digital banner – Animated web placement

The idea

Winsight, the parent company of Technomic, also produced events, including Menu Directions, a conference for public-sector foodservice. The stakeholders wanted to develop a new style of ad which could be run year-after-year with new messaging to keep it fresh.

The creation

After rounds of brainstorming, the idea of a “knowledge pillar” became favored, with the annual conference theme and marketing focus for each ad being separated by color. I also wanted to differentiate each message with color and hand-drawn lines to ensure each ad felt unique.

The media

These ads were run in print industry magazines, and animated digital banners were created for media site advertisements.
