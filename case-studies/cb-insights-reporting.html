Automated sector reports

Turning up the volume of information design output through automation.

Roles
Lead designer, design technologist
Tools
PowerPoint, Looker, Python
Team
1 data engineer, 1 designer/UX engineer
Timeline
May 2022 - January 2023

Overview

CB Insights needed a way to increase information-design output without losing the distinctive, hand-crafted feel of its reports.

Goal

Automate the path from structured data to editable, branded PowerPoint reports while keeping charts clear, consistent, and ready for human review.

Design the reports

The visual system balanced CB Insights’ digital-nostalgic colors and accent elements with clear table styles, editable charts, and guidelines for labels, axes, and dense time-series data.

Structure the data

I worked with a data scientist to define the report structure and wrote the first Python automation to turn prepared data into repeatable report content.

Export and refine

The export process generated PowerPoint pages and applied styles for colors, font sizes, and layout. Label-placement logic adjusted positions and sizes to avoid overlap while preserving readability.

Automation made more output possible without making the work feel automated.
