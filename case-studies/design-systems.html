Timeline
Ongoing

Overview

Sourcescrub sought a visual upgrade from its previous product experience. This work established scalable, usable, trusted design components and tokens for the product team.

Goal

Create a shared foundation that gives designers and developers a reliable source of truth while keeping complex interfaces easy to build and maintain.

Approach

The system moved the team away from one-off decisions toward reusable components, documented patterns, and a shared vocabulary for product work.

Design and tokens

Components were designed in Figma and informed by Angular Material. Tokens were organized across primitive values, system styles for spacing and type, and component-specific combinations that could be overridden when needed.

Storybook

Storybook became the working reference for the library. Stories documented component behavior, variants, and links to relevant design guidance so the whole team could contribute to the system.

A design system is only useful when the whole team can find, understand, and trust it.
