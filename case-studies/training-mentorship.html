Overview

A design system or platform tool is only as successful as the team’s ability to use it. I provide hands-on training, interactive workshops, and mentorship tailored to meet teams exactly where they are.

Goal

Bridge the gap between design theory and day-to-day execution, building lasting design literacy across engineering, product management, and content creation teams.

Custom Curriculum

Sessions range from deep-dive token architecture for senior front-end engineers to foundational UX and layout principles for product managers and marketers who build customer-facing collateral.

Hands-On Workshops

Rather than passive slide lectures, workshops focus on real company challenges. Teams refactor active UI screens, implement token hierarchies, and resolve design debt in real time.

Sustained Impact

Post-training documentation, office hours, and asynchronous feedback loops ensure that newly adopted practices become permanent organizational habits.

Design maturity grows when everyone on the team has the vocabulary and tools to build with care.
